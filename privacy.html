Privacy policy

rclone MMMBot is used by its owner alone, to copy files from the owner's own server into the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It requests one Google permission, drive.file: access only to the files it creates. It cannot read any other file in the Drive.
  • The files it uploads are the owner's own research data. They stay in the owner's Drive.
  • It collects no personal data about anyone. It shares nothing with anyone, and sells nothing.
  • The sign-in token is kept on the owner's own machines only.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated 29 October 2026.
